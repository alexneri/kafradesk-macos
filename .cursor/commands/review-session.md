# Session Review & Learning

## Objective

Let's pause and review this session. I want us to identify any missteps or incorrect assumptions from our work, analyze the lessons learned from them, and synthesize those insights into actionable coding or documentation principles.

## Key Question

What key lessons or missteps stand out to you that we can now use to establish new guiding assumptions for your planning and implementation logic?

## Process

**Important:** Do not provide a final list yet. Let's discuss your initial points first so we can explore them thoroughly.

## After Discussion

Once we have explored the points and reached conclusions, synthesize the agreed insights into actionable principles and write them to `~/Documents/DevContext/memory.md`:
- Coding or architectural principles → append to "Learned Patterns"
- Process or workflow corrections → append to "User Corrections"
- New knowledge about the codebase → append to "Repository Knowledge"

This ensures the lessons from this session carry forward into future planning and execution runs.
